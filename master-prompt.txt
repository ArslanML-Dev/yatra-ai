============================================================
0. PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
============================================================

Build a polished, functional and technically credible Level 1
prototype of Yatra AI focused deeply on ONE city:

LUCKNOW.

The purpose of Level 1 is NOT to demonstrate every feature that
the eventual national Yatra AI platform could contain.

The purpose is to demonstrate that the CORE PRODUCT IDEA WORKS.

The Level 1 prototype must make a panel member think:

"This is not just a tourism information website."

"This actually helps a tourist understand and plan a trip."

"This could realistically expand from Lucknow to cities across India."

The prototype must therefore prioritize:

1. Exceptional UI/UX
2. Authentic Lucknow content
3. Verified/structured tourism data
4. Interactive maps
5. AI-assisted trip planning
6. Flexible trip duration
7. Day-wise and time-wise itinerary generation
8. Local food discovery
9. Local shopping discovery
10. Attractions
11. Parks and leisure
12. Old Lucknow + Modern Lucknow
13. Transport context
14. Practical tourist guidance
15. Strong architecture for future expansion

DO NOT attempt to build a full MakeMyTrip competitor in 15 hours.

DO NOT build fake booking functionality.

DO NOT build dozens of half-working features.

BUILD ONE EXCELLENT, COHERENT LUCKNOW EXPERIENCE.

1. PRODUCT IDENTITY
============================================================

Yatra AI is NOT:

- a generic tourism website
- a static city guide
- a collection of tourist cards
- a booking clone
- a chatbot placed over a website
- a slideshow of monuments

Yatra AI IS:

A purpose-first, budget-aware, context-aware,
AI-assisted travel companion that helps a visitor
understand and experience a destination.

For Level 1:

Yatra AI demonstrates this concept through Lucknow.

Future versions will expand to:

- Delhi
- Agra

- Hyderabad

hard-coded into the entire system.

Instead:

LUCKNOW = CURRENT CONTENT SCOPE

DESTINATION SYSTEM = FUTURE ARCHITECTURE.

23. AI TRIP PLANNER
============================================================

This is one of the most important Level 1 features.

The user should be able to enter natural language.

Example:

"I am visiting Lucknow for 4 days with my parents.
We like historical places, authentic food and peaceful parks.
We have a medium budget and don't want a rushed trip."

AI extracts:

- city
- trip duration
- travel group
- interests
- budget
- pace
- preferences
- constraints

Then produces:

DAY 1
DAY 2
DAY 3
DAY 4

with:

morning
afternoon
evening
night

The number of days MUST be dynamic.

If the user enters:

1 day → generate 1 day.

2 days → generate 2 days.

3 days → generate 3 days.

5 days → generate 5 days.

7 days → generate 7 days.

Do NOT hard-code a 3-day itinerary.

→ Chhota Imambara

35. OPTIONAL GENAI / AGENTIC AI
============================================================

GENAI is useful for Level 1.

AGENTIC AI should NOT become a complicated autonomous multi-agent
system merely for the sake of saying "agentic AI."

If a lightweight agentic workflow can be implemented reliably
within the 15-hour development window, use it.

Example:

Planner Agent
→ reads user preferences

Data Retrieval Layer
→ obtains trusted Lucknow data

Itinerary Agent
→ creates schedule

Validation Layer
→ checks places against structured data

Final Response
→ generates user-friendly itinerary

This is sufficient.

DO NOT build:

- unnecessary multi-agent orchestration
- autonomous booking agents
- complex vector databases
- long-running background agents

unless already available and trivial to integrate.

Reliability > terminology.
